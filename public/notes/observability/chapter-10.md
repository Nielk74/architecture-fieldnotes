## Transfer challenge: A safe but unconvincing pilot

A manager suggests instrumenting a dormant service while an important API has unexplained weekly stalls.

### Pilot on the stalled API

Offers a concrete opportunity to demonstrate diagnostic value. Needs careful coordination with the responsible team. A solved stall supplies an investigation that the team has a reason to reuse.

### Pilot on the dormant service

Provides a simple environment for initial setup. May demonstrate none of the benefits needed to win adoption. The team learns installation steps but may see no reason to change its debugging practice.

Keep setup bounded, but choose a problem whose solution matters enough for colleagues to notice.
