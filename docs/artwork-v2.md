Art direction: sophisticated crisp vector-like editorial drawing, large elegant geometric curves and filled shapes, controlled facet shading, exquisite clean silhouettes, no heavy cartoon outlines, NOT a 3D clay render, NOT painterly, no texture noise. Color hierarchy: predominantly violet and midnight indigo environment, warm amber-orange wood and beavers, ivory robot and highlights. Only small cool-blue light accents. Restrained color families with rich light and dark values. Clear storytelling and beautiful scenic depth, not a pile of gadgets. No text, no lettering, no logos, no watermark, no border. Keep important subjects within the central 85% so it works on course cards. Original characters and original composition.
